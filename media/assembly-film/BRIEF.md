## Intent

An animated assembly of the whole JX1 humanoid we designed — not image by image: every CAD part flies into place
in build order, the camera follows the build, and the finished robot walks. The user asked: "i want animated..
assembly.. not just the image by image please make this", "anyone should be able to make robots", and for the
video to be made with HyperFrames.

## Assets

- assets/footage.mp4 — the 3D assembly footage rendered from the SolidWorks part meshes by
  tools/media/assembly_film.py (MuJoCo renderer, 1920x1080, 30 fps). The motion lives here.
- ../assembly/timeline.json — segment times (intro, explode, 22 steps, finale, walk) that the titles sync to.

## Customizations

- Step cards in sync with the footage (step n / 22, part names, real specs from the BOM/FEA), build-progress bar,
  opening title, "87 parts" explode beat, finale stats, "and it walks" title, end card with the repo URL.

## Notes

- Built autonomously on the user's "please make this" (no interview); no music or voiceover (not asked for).
- index.html is generated by tools/media/build_assembly_composition.py — edit the generator, not the HTML.
- Design: dark studio footage; League Gothic titles + IBM Plex Mono specs (both renderer-bundled); cyan #3cc8ff
  matches the parts' arrival glow, orange #f26b1d is the JX1 head colour.
