## Intent

The user asked for the JX1 assembly video's equivalent for JX0: "like the assembly video of jx1 i need for jx0 also".
Every SolidWorks part of JX0 flies into place in the order of the build guide, the camera follows the build, and the
finished robot walks its verified gait.

## Assets

- assets/footage.mp4 — 3D assembly footage rendered from the SolidWorks STL meshes (jx0/cad/stl) by
  tools/media/assembly_film_jx0.py (MuJoCo renderer, 1920x1080, 30 fps). The motion lives here.
- ../jx0_assembly/timeline.json — segment times (intro, explode, 13 steps, finale, walk) the titles sync to.

## Customizations

- Same design system as the JX1 film: step cards (step n / 13, section, real specs), build-progress bar, opening
  title, parts-count explode beat, finale stats, "and it walks" title, end card with the repo URL.

## Notes

- index.html is generated by tools/media/build_assembly_composition.py --robot jx0 — edit the generator, not the HTML.
- No music or voiceover (not asked for, as for JX1).
- Design: dark studio footage; League Gothic titles + IBM Plex Mono specs; cyan #3cc8ff matches the parts' arrival
  glow, orange #f26b1d is the JX0 brand dot (as in the cost estimate PDF).
