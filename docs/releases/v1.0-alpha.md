# JX1 v1.0-alpha — the complete design package (2026-09-24)

**Alpha** means the whole robot is designed, analysed and simulated, but **not built yet**. Everything needed to start
building is in this release. Some numbers will change once real parts are measured; the limitations are listed below.

![JX1](../images/jx1_cover.png)

## What is in it

| Area | Contents |
|---|---|
| CAD | 46 native parametric SolidWorks parts, the 87-component robot, `JX1_LowerBody` / `JX1_UpperBody` / `JX1_Robot` assemblies with limit-mated joints |
| Manufacturing | 24 STEP files, 14 A3 PDF drawings, 24 PETG fit-check STLs, 3 print STLs (`manufacturing/`) |
| Verification | every joint moved through its range in SolidWorks and checked for collisions; overhead arm range checked in MuJoCo on the CAD shapes (49/49 agreement with SolidWorks); voxel FEA of every structural part (all pass in aluminium) |
| Cost | [BOM](../../bom/master_bom.csv) with Indian suppliers: **₹7,12,481**, **₹8,19,353** with 15 % contingency; 9-page [cost estimate PDF](../../bom/JX1_cost_estimate.pdf) |
| Assembly | [22-step assembly guide](../assembly_guide.md) with pictures, [96-second assembly film](../../media/jx1_assembly.mp4), [build guide](../build_guide.md) |
| Electronics | [wiring](../electrical_wiring.md): power tree, 6 CAN buses, dual-path E-stop; Teensy 4.1 hub firmware (500 Hz, watchdogs, total motor-torque cap at 80 % of peak) |
| Simulation | MuJoCo model generated from the CAD (33.61 kg), URDF + xacro, Isaac Sim import |
| Walking controller | default `jx1_walk_stand_v7` (reinforcement learning): see below; ROS 2 packages (description, simulation, policy, bringup, hardware bridge), hardware-in-the-loop twin of the hub firmware |

## Key numbers (simulation of the CAD model; CALCULATED)

| | |
|---|---|
| Size | 1.23 m, 33.6 kg, 23 joints (legs 12, waist 1, arms 8, neck 2) |
| Walking | trained up to 0.8 m/s (0.75–0.77 m/s reached; 0.68 m/s through the emulated hub chain with its torque caps); side-steps ±0.39 m/s; turns up to 0.87 rad/s |
| Robustness | 118/130 (flat) and 119/130 (rough) commands tracked with 0 falls; survives 29–60 N·s pushes walking, 26–55 N·s standing |
| Standing | still (0 foot lift-offs), 73 W |
| Battery (468 Wh) | 5.1 h standing, 1.37 h walking at 0.5 m/s, 1.08 h at 0.8 m/s; peak current 15.9 A (BMS 40 A) |
| Strength | every structural part: safety factor 1.77–14.4 static, 1.58–24.7 fatigue (required 1.5) |

## Why "alpha": known limitations

- **Nothing is built or measured yet.** All performance numbers come from simulation.
- **Actuator bolt patterns are ASSUMED** until the official RobStride drawings or a measured unit are in the CAD (OI-1).
  Do the PETG fit-check prints before ordering metal.
- **Metal-part prices are ESTIMATED** from job-shop rates; get quotes (OI-12).
- **Whole-robot SolidWorks re-check after the arm redesign is pending** (OI-25; the legs and upper body were each
  re-verified).
- **Ankle motors are the tightest actuators** at 0.8 m/s (they reach the 80 % hub cap); bench-check them before fast
  walking (OI-21).
- **Bolted plate joints**: design loads and bolt/dowel sizing done; fastener geometry in CAD and a prototype strain
  check to go (OI-17).
- **Slow forward walking undershoots** (84 % of 0.3 m/s through the hardware-in-the-loop chain).
- **Isaac Lab / Isaac Sim** task is offline-checked only, not run (UNVERIFIED).
- **Drawings** come from a SolidWorks Educational licence and carry its "For Instructional Use Only" stamp (OI-19).
  **No licence** has been chosen for the repository yet.

Full list: [open issues](../open_issues.md), [risk register](../risk_register.md).
